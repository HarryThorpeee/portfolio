Intro

I'm a Product Designer based in London. For the past four years I've worked across startup and agency environments, currently at EE, designing journeys inside Aimee, their AI assistant used by around 10 million customers.

Before that, I spent nearly three years at Shape History, designing websites and digital products for campaigns, charities, and NGOs, working with clients like WWF, The Big Give, and CIFF. These days the rabbit hole is AI. Designing Aimee all day made me want to understand it from the inside as well as the outside, so a fair amount of my own time now goes into building it into my process, and staying close enough to the tools to know which ones genuinely earn their place.

Outside of client work, I take on freelance projects, mostly mobile apps and brand work. Away from the screen, I DJ and I'm occasionally on the radio. Right now I'm training for a half marathon in September, and I'm a sucker for good cinema, live music, and the odd exhibition.
